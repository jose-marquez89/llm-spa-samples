STAGE 01 / 05 💡 Data Analogy: Categorical Encoding / Lookup Table

Tokenization: Chopping Text into Lookup IDs

Before an LLM can do any math, it chops your text into frequent chunks called tokens. Common words stay whole, while rare words get split into reusable prefixes and suffixes. Every token is mapped to a fixed integer ID in a ~100,000-row vocabulary lookup table.

📊 Why Data People Know This

Just like SQL databases or regression models can't multiply raw strings like "New York", LLMs replace text chunks with categorical integer IDs (ID #4821) from a fixed dictionary.

🤯 Why LLMs Struggle to Count Letters

Why did early models fail at "How many R's in strawberry?" Because the model never sees the letters s-t-r-a-w-b-e-r-r-y! It only sees two integer IDs: [straw] + [berry].

STAGE 02 / 05 💡 Data Analogy: Multi-Column Feature Row & PCA Scatter Plot

Embeddings: Turning Token IDs into Coordinates in Meaning Space

Token ID #4821 is just an arbitrary label—ID #4821 isn't "closer" to ID #4822. To capture meaning, the LLM looks up each token in a giant spreadsheet where every word has thousands of numerical feature columns (e.g., Is it royalty? Is it tech? Is it plural?). Words with similar meanings live close together in this coordinate space!

📊 The Spreadsheet Intuition

Imagine a table with 100,000 rows (words) and 3,072 numeric columns (hidden traits scored from -1.0 to +1.0). That row of numbers is a word's Embedding Vector.

🧭 Why You Can Do Math on Words

Because every concept is a coordinate in space, directions have meaning! Moving along the "Gender" axis or "Country-to-Capital" axis lets you add and subtract concepts like numbers.

STAGE 03 / 05 💡 Data Analogy: Dynamic Relational Join + Weighted Average

Self-Attention: How Context Changes a Word’s Meaning

In Stage 2, the word "bank" had a single static coordinate. But does "bank" mean a financial institution or a muddy river edge? The Transformer Self-Attention layer lets every word look at all other words in the sentence, score their relevance, and compute a weighted average to update its own coordinates!

📊 Weighted Average Intuition

When processing the word "it", the model calculates attention weights (summing to 100%) across earlier words: e.g., 0.62 × [trophy] + 0.24 × [fit] + 0.14 × [other].

🔀 Multi-Head Attention

Just like running multiple SQL joins at once—one head tracks pronoun references (who is "it"?), another tracks verb-object relationships, and another tracks modifiers.

STAGE 04 / 05 💡 Data Analogy: Multinomial Logistic Regression & Weighted Dice Roll

Next-Token Prediction: Probabilities, Temperature & Top-P

After passing through dozens of Attention layers, the model produces a raw score (logit) for every single token in its 100,000-word vocabulary. It converts those scores into a probability distribution summing to 100% (via Softmax) and samples ONE next token. Then it appends that token to your prompt and repeats the entire loop!

🌡️ What "Temperature" Actually Does

Before converting scores to percentages, the model divides logits by Temperature (T). At T = 0, the #1 most likely token always wins (deterministic). At T > 1.0, the distribution flattens so creative/surprising words get a chance.

✂️ What "Top-P (Nucleus Sampling)" Does

Sorts candidates from highest to lowest probability and keeps only the top tokens whose cumulative probability reaches P% (e.g. 85%), zeroing out the bizarre "long tail" to prevent gibberish.

STAGE 05 / 05 💡 Data Analogy: Base Model Fitting vs. Business Rule Calibration

From Internet Autocomplete to ChatGPT, Claude & Gemini

A freshly trained LLM (the Base Model) only knows how to autocomplete internet documents. If you ask a Base Model "How do I calculate customer lifetime value?", it might reply with "Question 2: How do I calculate churn?" because it thinks it's autocompleting a textbook exam! Here is how labs turn a raw predictor into a helpful assistant.

PHASE 1: PRE-TRAINING

🌍 Self-Supervised Next-Token Prediction

Trained on trillions of words of books, articles, and code. Hide the last word of a sentence, ask the model to guess it, and nudge the billions of weights slightly closer to the truth.

Compute: ~98% of total budget (Months on 10,000+ GPUs/TPUs)
PHASE 2: SFT (INSTRUCTION TUNING)

👩‍🏫 Supervised Fine-Tuning

Human domain experts write 100,000+ high-quality (User Prompt → Ideal Assistant Response) pairs. The model learns the format of answering questions helpfully rather than just autocompleting web pages.

Analogy: Calibration on curated gold-standard labeled data
PHASE 3: RLHF & REASONING RL

🏆 Preference Alignment & Thinking

The model generates two answers (A and B). Humans or Constitutional principles rank which one is clearer, safer, and more accurate. Modern models also practice step-by-step "Chain of Thought" math & coding verification.

Analogy: A/B Testing + Reward Optimization Loop
